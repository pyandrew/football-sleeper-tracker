The League of Ordinary Gentlemen

Rules and Regulations

Keepers

Our league is a hybrid dynasty/keeper format. You can keep up to 20 players (the maximum possible setting for keepers on Sleeper). Keepers will cost you increasing draft picks each time.

  • Keeping a player for the first time will cost you a draft pick one spot higher than where you drafted them this season. Waiver wire pickups can be kept for an 8th round draft pick.
  • Keeping a player a second consecutive time will cost you a draft pick three spots higher than where you drafted them this season.
  • Keeping a player a third consecutive time will cost you a draft pick five spots higher than where you drafted them this season.
  • Keeping a player a fourth consecutive time will cost you a draft pick seven spots higher than where you drafted them this season. This is, my nature of the size of the draft, the maximum number of times a player can be kept.

If a player is traded, the draft picks required to keep them are transferred to the new manager and not reset.

If two players would require the same round of draft pick to keep, both can be kept by using a draft pick one round higher to keep the second player. The player who has been kept more consecutive years will be the one placed in the higher round draft pick slot (and will be randomized if both players have been kept the same number of times). If a manager happens to have two draft picks for the same round available to use for these keepers (by trading), both will be used for the keeper. If a manager has traded away their draft pick that would be required to keep a player, a draft pick one round higher will be needed.

Draft

The draft will be a snake draft in our first season. After that, the draft will be a linear, non-snaking draft. A draft lottery will determine the order for the six teams that missed the playoffs the previous season (all six will have equal odds, to discourage tanking). The third place team will pick 7th, the fourth place team will pick 8th, the runner-up will pick 9th, and the defending champion will pick 10th.

Roster

The bench size will be larger compared to our ESPN league to accommodate space to hold on to potential keepers while also actively managing the roster. The positions are:

Starters: 1 QB, 2 RB, 3 WR, 1 TE, 1 D/ST, 1 K

Bench: 9 spots

Injured Reserve: 2 spots. Players who are Out, Doubtful, Suspended, NA, DNR/Holdout/Opt-out are not IR eligible.

Scoring

There are changes to the scoring system based on new options available on Sleeper. Here is a summary of the changes:

Passing: You can now gain 0.25 points for getting a passing first down or a rushing first down, but can also now lose 0.5 points for getting sacked.

Rushing: Rushing first downs now gain 0.25 points.

Receiving: Receiving first downs now gain 0.5 points. As our league is a half PPR league, this makes it now a full PPR league whenever players are actually moving the chains.

Fumbles: Players will lose 1 point for a fumble, and an additional 1 points if the fumble is lost.

Kicking: Field goals are default set to 3 points. There are now fractional points for every yard kicked above 30 yards. This means, for example, a 49 yard field goal is worth 4.9 points. Missing a FG from 0-39 yards will give -2 points, missing a FG from 40-49 will give -1 points, and missing from 50+ will have no penalty. PAT is 1 point and missing a PAT is -1 points.

Defense: Our scoring will be set to the system outlined in the post linked below, with a few small modifications. 4th down stops will be set to gain 2 points instead of 1 (making it the same as any turnover), and passes defended will be set to 0.25 points.

https://www.reddit.com/r/SleeperApp/comments/184el0f/optimal_dst_scoring_proposal/

Transactions

Waivers will process at 9pm Eastern each day.

The league commissioner will have the sole vote to veto any trades (this veto will only be used for trades that are collusive in nature, beyond a reasonable doubt). If a trade seems suspicious to you, reach out to your league commissioner to alert them to take a closer look.

Draft picks are tradeable.

Future Rule Changes

Wherever possible, future changes to the rules will be grandfathered in. This will be true for keeper rules. A player previously drafted will be run by the keeper rules from when they were drafted, and changes to the keeper rules will only apply to newly drafted players.

Clarifications

Dropped players (Nov 2026): I didn't write the ruleset clear enough on dropped players and their keeper status so I wanted to make sure the rule is clarified ahead of the keeper portion of the offseason.

Dropped players maintain their draft position status. The 8th round pick to keep a waiver player only applies to undrafted players. If I drafted someone in the 15th round and dropped him, he can be kept by whoever picks him up for a 14th rounder.
